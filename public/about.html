Built to reduce security noise

Threat Pulse is a security-information publication of Calculated Ventures, LLC. It brings together public-source threat intelligence, free tools, and practical security guidance. Our RMF Accelerator and professional services are separate offerings.

Editorial standards

We use primary sources where possible, distinguish advisory data from interpretation, and avoid invented risk scores. The feed is automatically sourced from the CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog; entries are displayed with source links, and any data outage should be treated as a reason to consult CISA directly.

Calculator outputs are estimates, not guarantees or expert assessments. This publication is not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA, CISA, NIST, or any U.S. government agency. See Calculated Ventures for the parent company and commercial services.
