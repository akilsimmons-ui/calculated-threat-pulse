What your team should do with this list

  1. Confirm affected product, supported fix, and vulnerable versions in the vendor advisory.
  2. Match the affected component against your asset inventory and actual exposure.
  3. Assign an accountable owner, an authorized remediation path, and a target date.
  4. Verify the change and preserve evidence before marking any finding resolved.
